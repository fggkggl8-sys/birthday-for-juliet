My dearest Juliet, ♡ Happiest Birthday, meri pyaari si Juliet! ❤️ Pata hai, I was sitting and thinking about what I should write for you. And then I realised something — tumhare liye kuch bhi likhun, woh thoda kam hi lagega. Because how do I put into words what it feels like to have someone like you in my life? So I'm not going to try to sound perfect. I'm just going to tell you what's in my heart. First of all, HAPPY BIRTHDAYYYYY, MY GIRL! 🥹💕 Aaj ka din sirf ek date nahi hai. It's the day someone very, very special came into this world. Someone who has this beautiful way of making ordinary moments feel unforgettable. Someone whose smile can make a bad day feel a little lighter. And yes, someone whose cute, silly expressions deserve their own fan club. Juliet, tumhe shayad kabhi kabhi realise bhi nahi hota ki tum kitni special ho. Tumhari woh chhoti chhoti baatein, tumhara hasna, tumhari funny faces banana, aur woh random moments jab hum bas baat karte rehte hain — I carry all of that with me. Even after a call ends, the happiness doesn't just disappear. It stays. Kabhi socha hai? Do log alag alag jagah hote hue bhi ek dusre ke itne close kaise feel kar sakte hain? I don't know the exact answer. But I know that when it comes to you, distance is just a number on a map. My heart doesn't really understand kilometres. It only understands that I miss you. And haan, I wish I could be right there today. I wish I could see you cut your cake, steal a little frosting, tease you for being the birthday girl, and watch that smile in real life instead of through a screen. Main tumhare saath hota toh shayad tumhe itna tang karta ki tum bolti, 'Bas bhi karo!' 😂 But I'd still be the happiest person in the room, because I'd be there with you. There are so many little things I want to do with you someday. Walk beside you without having to worry about a call ending. Take silly photos. Eat something delicious and argue about who gets the last bite. Celebrate birthdays together, not just through messages and screens. Simple things, you know? But with you, even simple things sound like a dream. One thing I really, really admire about you is your heart. The way you've shared happiness with others, the way you've given something of yourself to make someone else's day better — that says so much about who you are. Beauty isn't only about how someone looks, Juliet. Sometimes it's in what they do when they have a chance to be kind. And you have that kind of beauty too. Mujhe tum par genuinely proud feel hota hai. Not because you have to be perfect. Not because you always have to be happy. But because you're you. Even on the days when you feel tired, confused, or not your best, you still deserve gentleness, care, and so much love. Please never forget that. On your birthday, I don't want to wish you only big achievements or fancy things. I wish you peaceful sleep after difficult days. I wish you people who understand you. I wish you the courage to chase every dream that matters to you. I wish you laughter that makes your stomach hurt, tiny surprises on ordinary afternoons, and moments when you stop and think, 'Life is actually beautiful.' Aur ek selfish si wish meri bhi hai… ki aane wale saalon mein tumhari bahut saari happy memories ka ek chhota sa hissa main bhi ban paun. I want to keep discovering new things about you. Your favourite little habits. The things that make you laugh. The dreams you haven't told anyone yet. The version of you that feels safe enough to be completely herself. Thank you for the smiles, for the conversations, for the little moments that probably seem ordinary to you but mean so much to me. Thank you for being wonderfully, beautifully, adorably Juliet. And listen, birthday girl — aaj zyada tension nahi leni. Smile karna hai, cake enjoy karna hai, apna favourite khana khana hai, aur khud ko special feel karna hai. Because you ARE special. Bohottttt zyada. Samjhi? 💗 If I could give you one gift that never runs out, it would be the feeling of being loved. The kind of love that doesn't disappear on difficult days. The kind that cheers for you when you win and sits quietly beside you when things are hard. Maybe this website is just a little collection of pictures, stars, hearts, and words. But every part of it carries one simple message: You matter to me. So much. So here's to you, Juliet. To your kindness, your laughter, your dreams, your silly faces, your beautiful heart, and every lovely chapter still waiting to happen. Happy Birthday once again, meri jaan. I hope you smile when you read this. And if you do, then my little surprise has already done its job. Aaj, kal, aur har us din jab tumhe ek reminder chahiye ho — remember that somewhere, someone is smiling just because you exist. I love you, Juliet. More than these words can ever explain. With a heart full of love, a thousand imaginary hugs, and one very big birthday kiss, Forever your Romeo. ♡
